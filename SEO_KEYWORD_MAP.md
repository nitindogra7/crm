No standalone `/form-backend`, generic `/contact-form-backend`, or separate framework variant pages were added. The homepage and useful integration guides own these overlapping intents. Framework and old query aliases permanently redirect to the final guide. A static-site form is supported; named website-builder integrations and SDKs are not claimed without verification.

Comparison boundary: Formspree has a submission inbox, CSV/JSON exports, and external CRM plugins. Infraly's visible status-based lead workflow is a fit consideration, not evidence that competitors lack lead management. No numerical pricing advantage, security superiority, customer counts, or integration parity is claimed. Recheck the official sources before each material comparison update.
